# art/ — Codex deliveries

Empty in M1: the gray box draws everything with plain shapes (`src/game/palette.ts` holds the
eight colors and the color-blind glyphs from the art brief).

What goes here from M2 is listed in `docs/spec.md` §5 (the Codex art brief): Glimmer comets, the
orbit, the crescent Dock, stardust and star sprites, UI, and the chapter 1 pictures.

**Picture rule learned in M1:** a level's difficulty comes mostly from its picture. Pictures where
colors sit *behind* other colors (outlines, stripes, rings, spots inside a fill) make launch order
matter; big flat areas of one color make levels that can't be lost. Each picture should use only
the 8 game colors, at the exact grid size, with at least two colors that are hidden at the start.
