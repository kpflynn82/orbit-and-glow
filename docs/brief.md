# Orbit & Glow — one-page brief

**The game in five sentences.** A picture made of colored stardust hangs in the night sky inside
an oval orbit. You tap comets waiting on launch rails below; each one rides the orbit for one lap
and clears every speck of its own color that it can see from the edge. A comet with charge left
over parks in a small Dock, and a full Dock with nowhere to park means you're stuck. The board
never changes and nothing depends on how fast you tap, so every level is a fair puzzle about the
order you launch in. Clear the picture and its stardust turns to stars.

**The loop.** Choose a comet → watch it orbit and sweep its color → manage the Dock → light up the
picture.

**The twist (in this build since Oct 16, from level 6).** Fusion: drag two primary-colored comets
together in the Dock to make one secondary-colored comet with the charges added (Red 7 + Blue 4 =
Purple 11). Brought forward from M2 after Kevin's first playtest showed the base puzzle alone had
an obvious winning strategy.

**What makes it ours.** Night-sky observatory theme, an oval orbit of light, curved rails, a
crescent Dock, order-not-timing rules, fusion, and a fair money layer (no lives, no forced ads,
nothing sold at the moment of loss). Full originality check: `docs/spec.md` §2.

**M1 goal (this build — gray box).** Orbit, rails, Dock, order-only resolution, win and both kinds
of stuck, free retry, color-blind glyphs, 20 levels, and the level solver.
**Done when:** Kevin plays 20 levels on his phone; the solver agrees with every win and every
stuck; the solver finishes on a 16×16 board in seconds.

**Not in this build:** special comets (Twin, Veiled), hints, the Night Atlas, art, sound, money.
